Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Does QR Pro Scan collect my data?

No. QR Pro Scan does not collect, store, or transmit personal data. All scanning and QR generation happens locally on your device.

Does the app require an internet connection?

No. Most features work completely offline. Internet is only needed when opening external links.

Why can’t some QR codes be scanned?

Some QR codes may be damaged, low quality, or use unsupported formats. Ensure good lighting and a clear image when scanning.

Is QR Pro Scan free?

QR Pro Scan may offer both free and premium features depending on your version. Any pricing or subscriptions are clearly shown inside the app.

How can I restore purchases?

If applicable, use the “Restore Purchases” option inside the app settings. If issues persist, contact support.

Can I suggest new features?

Absolutely. We welcome feature requests and feedback via email.
