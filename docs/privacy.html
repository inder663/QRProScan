Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how QR Pro Scan (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

Last updated: January 2026

1. Information We Collect

QR Pro Scan is designed with privacy in mind. We do not collect or store personal data on our servers.

  • Scanned QR code content is processed locally on your device
  • Created QR codes are stored only on your device
  • Scan history remains local and never leaves your device

2. Camera Access

The app requires camera access solely to scan QR codes. Camera data is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

3. Photos Access (Optional)

If you choose to scan QR codes from images, the app accesses photos only when explicitly selected by you. Images are processed locally and never uploaded.

4. Internet Usage

QR Pro Scan may open links contained in QR codes using your device’s browser. We do not track, log, or monitor any websites you visit.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with third parties. There are no analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers in QR Pro Scan.

6. Children’s Privacy

QR Pro Scan does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

7. Security

We follow industry best practices to ensure your data remains secure on your device. Since no data is transmitted or stored remotely, your privacy risk is minimal.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
